    How to Play

    • Goal: Cut the required percentage of grass on each lawn before you run out of fuel/battery or time.
    • Move: WASD or the Arrow keys. On touch devices, use the on-screen pad.
    • Cut: Just drive over uncut grass. Wider decks cut more in one pass.
    • Energy: Petrol mowers burn fuel; cordless mowers drain a battery. Drive over the refuel pad to top up.
    • Avoid: Trees, ponds, flowerbeds and gnomes. Hitting an obstacle stalls you and dents your tidiness bonus.
    • Earn: Finish lawns to earn £. Spend it in the Garage to unlock faster, wider, longer-lasting Hyundai machines.
